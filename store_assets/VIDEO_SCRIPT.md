# Promo video — shot list & 30-second script (7 languages)

A short promo video is optional on Play but lifts conversion. This is a
ready-to-record plan: capture the app on a real device (portrait, ~1080×1920,
60 fps), cut to the beats below, and lay the localized caption/VO over each.
Keep the palette calm (brand cream/gold) and the pace unhurried — the app's
appeal is the quiet "aha", not frenetic motion. Total ≈ 30 s.

## Shot list

| # | Duration | On screen | Action to capture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–4 s | Home | Open app; the daily-puzzle card and menu settle in. |
| 2 | 4–12 s | Puzzle board | Tap **Play**; type 3–4 letters; a word locks in (the highlight). |
| 3 | 12–18 s | Puzzle solved | Final letter lands → the quote reads clean; solved banner. |
| 4 | 18–23 s | Stats / streak | Streak +1, calendar heatmap, a fast solve time. |
| 5 | 23–27 s | Packs / themes | Scroll difficulty + themed packs; flick light→dark theme. |
| 6 | 27–30 s | Icon / wordmark | End card: the "Q?" mark + "Quotecrack" + short CTA. |

Closing CTA card text = the app name + short description line (per language,
below). No price claims in the video.
