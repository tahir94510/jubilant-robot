# Privacy Policy — Quotecrack

_Last updated: July 15, 2026_

> The live, linkable version of this policy (use THIS url in Play Console):
> **https://tahir94510.github.io/quotecrack/privacy.html**
> (served from this repository's GitHub Pages site; the repository stays
> public so this link stays alive)

Quotecrack ("the app") is a cryptogram word-puzzle game published by an
independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app
handles and why. The short version: **the game itself works fully offline,
requires no account, and we never see your gameplay data.**

## Data the app stores on your device only

Your puzzle progress, statistics, streaks, settings, and hint balance are
stored locally on your device. They are never transmitted to us and are
deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Advertising (Google AdMob and mediation partners)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and
process data described in [Google's partner sites
policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), including
your device's **Advertising ID**, IP address, coarse location derived from
IP, and ad-interaction data (for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud
prevention, and — where you consent — personalization).

To improve ad quality, ads may also be served through AdMob mediation by
these partner networks, each processing similar data under its own policy:

- **AppLovin** — [privacy policy](https://legal.applovin.com/privacy/)
- **Unity Ads** — [privacy policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- **Pangle** — [privacy policy](https://www.pangleglobal.com/privacy/enduser-en)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland the app
shows a consent dialog (Google UMP, IAB TCF v2) before any ad is loaded;
your choices there apply to the mediation partners as well. You can change
your choice at any time via _Settings → Privacy options_ in the app. You
can also reset your device Advertising ID or opt out of personalization in
Android settings.

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional one-time Premium purchase is processed entirely by Google
Play. We do not receive your name, address, or payment details — only an
anonymous confirmation that the purchase is owned, stored on your device.
Premium also removes all advertising (and with it, all AdMob data
processing).

## Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled locally on your device. No data
leaves your device for it, and you can disable it in the app or in system
settings at any time.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Ads are configured as
non-child-directed. If you believe a child has provided data through the ad
network, please contact us.

## Data deletion

All app data lives on your device: uninstalling the app deletes it. Ad
consent choices can be withdrawn in-app. For AdMob data subject requests,
see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change and note the
date above.
