Data Deletion — Quotecrack

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Quotecrack has no accounts and no server: everything the game knows about you (puzzle progress, streaks, statistics, settings, hint tokens) is stored only on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, so there is no server-side copy for us to delete — you are always in full control.

Delete all game data

Either of these removes every piece of data the app stores, permanently and immediately:

  • Uninstall the app — Android deletes all of its local data with it; or
  • Keep the app but wipe its data: Android Settings → Apps → Quotecrack → Storage & cache → Clear storage.

There is no retention period: once deleted on your device, the data is gone. We hold no copy.

Advertising identifier

The free version shows ads via Google AdMob, which uses the Android advertising ID. That identifier is managed by Android, not by us — you can delete or reset it at Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, and Google's handling of it is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store payment data.
